# EventStream SLA v2

This agreement supersedes v1 for EventStream from March 2026 onward.

## Scope
This agreement covers EventStream and the Data Platform team.

## Latency Target
Under v2, EventStream targets a p99 latency of 20ms.

## Error Budget
Under v2, the monthly error budget is 0.1%.
