# PaymentGateway SLA v2

This agreement supersedes v1 for PaymentGateway from March 2026 onward.

## Scope
This agreement covers PaymentGateway and the Payments team.

## Latency Target
Under v2, PaymentGateway targets a p99 latency of 33ms.

## Error Budget
Under v2, the monthly error budget is 0.2%.
