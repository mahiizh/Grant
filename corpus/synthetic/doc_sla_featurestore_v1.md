# FeatureStore SLA v1

This agreement governed FeatureStore before the March 2026 revision.

## Scope
This agreement covers FeatureStore and the ML Platform team.

## Latency Target
Under v1, FeatureStore targets a p99 latency of 100ms.

## Error Budget
Under v1, the monthly error budget is 0.3%.
