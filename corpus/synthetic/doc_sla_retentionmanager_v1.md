# RetentionManager SLA v1

This agreement governed RetentionManager before the March 2026 revision.

## Scope
This agreement covers RetentionManager and the Compliance team.

## Latency Target
Under v1, RetentionManager targets a p99 latency of 152ms.

## Error Budget
Under v1, the monthly error budget is 0.7%.
