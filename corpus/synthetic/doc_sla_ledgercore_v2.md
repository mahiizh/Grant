# LedgerCore SLA v2

This agreement supersedes v1 for LedgerCore from March 2026 onward.

## Scope
This agreement covers LedgerCore and the Core Banking team.

## Latency Target
Under v2, LedgerCore targets a p99 latency of 46ms.

## Error Budget
Under v2, the monthly error budget is 0.3%.
