# TicketRouter SLA v1

This agreement governed TicketRouter before the March 2026 revision.

## Scope
This agreement covers TicketRouter and the Customer Support team.

## Latency Target
Under v1, TicketRouter targets a p99 latency of 113ms.

## Error Budget
Under v1, the monthly error budget is 0.4%.
