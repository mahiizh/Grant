# RetentionManager SLA v2

This agreement supersedes v1 for RetentionManager from March 2026 onward.

## Scope
This agreement covers RetentionManager and the Compliance team.

## Latency Target
Under v2, RetentionManager targets a p99 latency of 137ms.

## Error Budget
Under v2, the monthly error budget is 0.5%.
