# LedgerCore SLA v1

This agreement governed LedgerCore before the March 2026 revision.

## Scope
This agreement covers LedgerCore and the Core Banking team.

## Latency Target
Under v1, LedgerCore targets a p99 latency of 61ms.

## Error Budget
Under v1, the monthly error budget is 0.5%.
