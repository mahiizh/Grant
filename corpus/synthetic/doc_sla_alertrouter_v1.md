# AlertRouter SLA v1

This agreement governed AlertRouter before the March 2026 revision.

## Scope
This agreement covers AlertRouter and the Site Reliability Engineering team.

## Latency Target
Under v1, AlertRouter targets a p99 latency of 74ms.

## Error Budget
Under v1, the monthly error budget is 0.6%.
