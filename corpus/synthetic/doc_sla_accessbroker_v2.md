# AccessBroker SLA v2

This agreement supersedes v1 for AccessBroker from March 2026 onward.

## Scope
This agreement covers AccessBroker and the Security team.

## Latency Target
Under v2, AccessBroker targets a p99 latency of 72ms.

## Error Budget
Under v2, the monthly error budget is 0.5%.
