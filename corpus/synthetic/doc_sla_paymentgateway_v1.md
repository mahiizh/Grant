# PaymentGateway SLA v1

This agreement governed PaymentGateway before the March 2026 revision.

## Scope
This agreement covers PaymentGateway and the Payments team.

## Latency Target
Under v1, PaymentGateway targets a p99 latency of 48ms.

## Error Budget
Under v1, the monthly error budget is 0.4%.
