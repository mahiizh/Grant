# EventStream SLA v1

This agreement governed EventStream before the March 2026 revision.

## Scope
This agreement covers EventStream and the Data Platform team.

## Latency Target
Under v1, EventStream targets a p99 latency of 35ms.

## Error Budget
Under v1, the monthly error budget is 0.3%.
