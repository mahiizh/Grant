# AlertRouter SLA v2

This agreement supersedes v1 for AlertRouter from March 2026 onward.

## Scope
This agreement covers AlertRouter and the Site Reliability Engineering team.

## Latency Target
Under v2, AlertRouter targets a p99 latency of 59ms.

## Error Budget
Under v2, the monthly error budget is 0.4%.
