# AccessBroker SLA v1

This agreement governed AccessBroker before the March 2026 revision.

## Scope
This agreement covers AccessBroker and the Security team.

## Latency Target
Under v1, AccessBroker targets a p99 latency of 87ms.

## Error Budget
Under v1, the monthly error budget is 0.7%.
