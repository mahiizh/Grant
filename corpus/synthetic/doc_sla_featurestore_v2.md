# FeatureStore SLA v2

This agreement supersedes v1 for FeatureStore from March 2026 onward.

## Scope
This agreement covers FeatureStore and the ML Platform team.

## Latency Target
Under v2, FeatureStore targets a p99 latency of 85ms.

## Error Budget
Under v2, the monthly error budget is 0.1%.
