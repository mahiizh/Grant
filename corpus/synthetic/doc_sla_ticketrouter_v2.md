# TicketRouter SLA v2

This agreement supersedes v1 for TicketRouter from March 2026 onward.

## Scope
This agreement covers TicketRouter and the Customer Support team.

## Latency Target
Under v2, TicketRouter targets a p99 latency of 98ms.

## Error Budget
Under v2, the monthly error budget is 0.2%.
